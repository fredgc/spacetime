Spacetime Drawing Tool (v{{ VERSION }})

The Spacetime Drawing Tool is an interactive application designed to teach and visualize special relativity thought experiments.

It helps you construct spacetime diagrams with worldlines, events, light cones, and planes of simultaneity, such as those presented in Einstein’s book Relativity.

This application is written in Dart with Flutter—the source code is available on GitHub at https://github.com/fredgc/spacetime.

Found a bug or have a feature request? File an issue on GitHub.

The previous version (v5) was written in JavaScript. You can still run the Version 5 Drawing Tool, and audio recordings of lessons from version 5 (not yet ported to version 6) can be found at Version 5 Help.

Other apps and drawing tools can be found at www.gchouse.org.

Privacy and Data Retention

This program allows you to customize settings that are saved locally on your device (using SharedPreferences / LocalStorage). No personal data is sent to or stored on the gchouse.org server.

On Android, local settings are deleted when you uninstall the app. On Web, settings are deleted when you clear your browser’s local application data.

Drawings may be downloaded to your local device or optionally saved to and opened from your Google Drive account via Google Sign-In. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.
