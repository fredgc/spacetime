Privacy Policy for Spacetime Drawing Tool

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Zero-PII & Privacy by Design

The Spacetime Drawing Tool (“the App”) is an interactive educational application designed to construct, explore, and visualize special relativity thought experiments. We adhere to strict Privacy by Design: we do not collect personal information, we do not require account registration to use the tool, we do not track you across websites, and we do not sell or monetize user data.

This application is built for students, teachers, researchers, and relativity enthusiasts of all ages.

Information We Do NOT Collect

To maintain a zero-PII (Personally Identifiable Information) architecture: * No Mandatory User Accounts: You can create, edit, animate, and download spacetime diagrams without registering an account. * No Device Sensors or Invasive Permissions: The App does not access device location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, or biometric sensors. * No Advertising or Cross-Site Tracking: The App is completely ad-free and contains no advertising networks, marketing trackers, or commercial analytics SDKs.

Data Handled by the Application

1. Local Settings & Preferences

  • Settings such as your preferred theme mode (light, dark, or system), grid visibility, animation playback speed, and transformation view options are stored exclusively on your device using browser localStorage (on the Web) or SharedPreferences (on Android/Desktop).
  • On Web, clearing your browser application data deletes these preferences. On Android, uninstalling the app removes all stored preferences.

2. Diagram Files & Storage

  • Local Device Storage: Spacetime diagram files (.json) can be downloaded and opened directly on your local device.
  • Optional Google Drive Integration: If you choose to connect your Google Drive account to save or open diagrams, the App uses the official Google Drive API solely to access files created by or explicitly opened with this App. No diagram contents, credentials, or file metadata are ever transmitted to or stored on gchouse.org servers.
  • Shared Links: If you share a diagram URL containing serialized scene data, that data is encoded directly into the URL fragment and processed locally by the recipient’s browser.

Third-Party Infrastructure

The App is hosted statically using Google Firebase Hosting (Google LLC): * Standard technical connection metadata (such as IP addresses and HTTP request headers) is processed temporarily by Google Cloud infrastructure strictly for routing, security, and denial-of-service prevention. * For more information on Google’s privacy practices, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Children & Classroom Safe (COPPA & FERPA)

The App is designed to be fully compliant with educational privacy regulations: * COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act): The App does not collect personal information from children under 13. * FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act): Because no student education records or personal identifiers are collected, maintained, or shared, the App can be safely used in K-12 and university classroom environments.
