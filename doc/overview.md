---
title: Spacetime Overview
---
The Spacetime Drawing Tool features a split-screen view that shows relativity
from two different perspectives:

## 1. Spacetime Diagram (Top View)

The top canvas displays a **Spacetime Diagram** with one dimension of space
($x$ on the horizontal axis) and one dimension of time ($t$ on the vertical
axis).

* **Location** or **Worldlines**: Represent moving entities over time. A
  vertical line represents a stationary object, a tilted line represents a
  moving object, and a line tilted at 45 degrees represents something moving at
  the speed of light ($c = 1.0$).

* **Events**: Individual points representing occurrences at a specific
  coordinate $(x, t)$.

* **Instant** or **Planes of Simultaneity**: Represent space as perceived at a
  single instant of time by a given observer. Using a Newtonian transformation,
  these are horizontal, but with the Lorentz transformation they are not
  horizontal.

* **Light Cones**: Show the propagation of light outward from an event.

## 2. Observer View / Side View (Bottom View)

The bottom canvas displays the **Observer View** (also called the **Side
View**). It shows a spatial representation of what the observer sees at the
current instant of time ($t$), as selected by the Time slider.

* **Length Contraction**: Moving objects appear contracted in width along their
  direction of motion relative to the observer, computed using the relative
  velocity addition formula and the Lorentz factor.

* **Time Dilation**: Clocks on moving objects run slower compared to the
  observer's clocks.

* **Relativity of Simultaneity**: Events that are simultaneous in one frame
  occur at different times in a moving frame, represented by the tilt of the
  simultaneity plane.
