## 2. Educational & Pedagogical Purpose Only

The Spacetime Drawing Tool is an interactive educational application designed
to illustrate concepts in Albert Einstein's theory of Special Relativity,
including Minkowski spacetime diagrams, light cones, reference frame
transformations (Lorentz boosts), time dilation, and the relativity of
simultaneity:

* The App is intended solely for personal, academic, classroom, and
  pedagogical exploration.

* It is not designed or certified for professional aerospace engineering,
  satellite relativistic correction (such as GNSS/GPS clock synchronization),
  particle accelerator guidance, orbital mechanics, or mission-critical
  physics modeling.

## 3. Mathematical & Numerical Modeling

All geometric transformations, hyperbola calibrations, rapidity parameters,
and Lorentz boost animations are calculated numerically based on idealized
flat spacetime models (special relativity without gravitational curvature):

* Real-world gravitational perturbations, general relativistic frame dragging,
  and cosmological curvature are not modeled.

* The authors make no warranties regarding the quantitative suitability of the
  software for experimental physics verification.
